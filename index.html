About Me

I am a Video Game Design and Development graduate from the Centre de la Imatge i la Tecnologia Multimèdia (CITM) - Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya (UPC), focused on game programming with Unity and C#. My experience includes a six-month programming internship at SkillBack, over a year developing Shodoe with a team of eight, and two mobile puzzle games published on Google Play as the sole programmer.

I work with C#, C++, C, Lua, SDL, and object-oriented programming, with additional knowledge of Python, Maya, and ZBrush. I also have experience integrating in-app purchases, advertising, and rewarded ads in mobile games.
